# Confirmed Pain Points — Costco Customers

## Source
AI-assisted research across 13 public sources (articles, app reviews, BBB complaints, Reddit, social media). See audit.md for full source list.

## Pain Points

| # | Pain Point | Who | Severity | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Terrible app & website experience** — App is "unusably bad," crashes during checkout, no Apple Wallet integration, can't search in-store inventory | All members (esp. digital-savvy) | Critical | Daily |
| 2 | **Favorite products disappear without warning** — Short-term vendor contracts cause beloved items to vanish permanently | Loyal repeat buyers | High | Monthly |
| 3 | **Overcrowded stores & chaotic parking** — Peak hours are overwhelming; parking lots feel dangerous; shoppers sometimes give up and go home | Weekend/evening shoppers | High | Weekly |
| 4 | **Produce quality decline & spoilage** — Items go bad within hours/days; significantly shorter shelf life reported 2025–2026 | Grocery buyers | High | Weekly |
| 5 | **Pushy third-party salespeople** — Aggressive reps for phones, solar, TV block aisles and interrupt shopping | All in-store shoppers | Medium | Weekly |
| 6 | **Online order fulfillment failures** — Orders not shipped, no tracking transparency, lengthy resolution times | Online shoppers | Critical | Occasionally |
| 7 | **Long return counter lines (outdoors)** — Poor queue design forces customers to wait outside in weather | Members making returns | Medium | Occasionally |
| 8 | **Membership scan friction at entry** — Redundant identity check slows entry; biometric scanners feel invasive | All members | Medium | Every visit |
| 9 | **Self-checkout removal & limited payment options** — Fewer express options; Visa-only credit card policy frustrates members | Quick-trip shoppers | Medium | Weekly |

## Key Patterns

- **Digital experience is the #1 critical gap** — The app/website is universally criticized across all review sources
- **Inventory unpredictability** creates emotional frustration (product loyalty broken repeatedly)
- **Physical experience friction** (parking, crowds, lines) compounds digital disappointment
- **Quality perception declining** — Produce spoilage is a newer trend (2025–2026)
- **Online channel is unreliable** — Fulfillment failures erode trust in Costco's e-commerce
